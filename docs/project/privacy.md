# Privacy

This page covers the website migrax.org. The Migrax tool itself sends nothing anywhere: it
only talks to the databases you point it at.

## What the site counts

When you accept cookies in the banner, the site loads Google Analytics. It records:

- the pages you open and how long you stay on them,
- the page or site you came from (for example a search engine or a link on another site),
- your approximate location (country and city, worked out from your IP address; Google
  Analytics does not store the address itself),
- your browser, operating system, screen size and language,
- your answer when you click 👍 or 👎 under "Was this page helpful?" (that box only appears
  after you accept).

This tells us how many people visit, which guides and blog articles they read, and how they
found the site. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold or shared with anyone
else. Google processes the data under its
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

When you reject cookies, Google Analytics is not loaded and nothing is counted.

## Cookies and stored settings

| Name | Set by | What for | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_ZEENTK4J2Q` | Google Analytics, only after you accept | Tell repeat visits apart from new ones | Up to 2 years |
| `__consent` and other `__`-prefixed entries | The site itself, in your browser's local storage | Remember your cookie choice, dark or light mode and similar settings | Until you clear them |

The banner's settings also list **GitHub**: with it, the site asks GitHub's API for the
repository's star and fork counts shown in the header. It sets no cookies.

## Changing your choice

Click **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page to open the banner again. You can also
delete the site's cookies in your browser's settings.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as its
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
describes.
